Research Scientist | PhD

I am currently a Senior Research Scientist at AstraZeneca (Top 10 global and U.S pharma), with over six years of experience building and deploying machine-learning systems in production. My background spans applied ML, large-scale data systems, and end-to-end model deployment, with hands-on experience in LLMs, reinforcement learning, agentic AI systems, and low-latency backend engineering.

While my primary role is in industry healthcare and life sciences, my long-term career focus is Machine Learning Engineering, particularly in building scalable, real-world AI systems that bridge modeling, infrastructure, and product.
